# SELE Project Status

## Frozen Scope

**发现页与心动盲盒核心链路：已冻结**

Frozen areas:

- Discover page structure, copy, controls, Heartbox card, door asset, remaining-count state, "正在发生", bottom navigation, and the approved near-black/deep-gray/cool-white visual system.
- Heartbox flow: discover entry, detail, text echo, echo success, progressive unlock from 3/5 through 5/5, waiting for mutual echo, putting a moment back, switching moments, and opening another moment.
- Heartbox content rules: text-only moments and echoes; no stranger-facing image, video, or audio upload or playback; audio is reserved for a future private-message relationship state.
- Existing progressive personality fragments, tags, text moments, unlock steps, and mock-data structure.

Do not proactively change the frozen structure, routes, interaction path, copy, button names, card hierarchy, colors, typography, spacing, corner radii, icons, door asset, unlock steps, mock-data structure, or content rules.

These areas may be changed only after an explicit request to unfreeze the Discover page, unfreeze Heartbox, or adjust a specifically named frozen module. Other pages may reuse frozen components, but that reuse must not modify the frozen experience.
